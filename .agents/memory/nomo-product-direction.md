---
name: NOMO product direction
description: Durable product and visual direction for the NOMO UI template studio.
---

NOMO is an AI-powered productivity and decision-making control center combining productivity insights, dummy Sui assets, trading automation, and the Lwazi assistant. Its product identity is a futuristic quant-finance or hedge-fund terminal operating autonomously under human supervision.

**Why:** The user wants the UI to feel techy, premium, trustworthy, and precise rather than like a generic productivity dashboard.

**How to apply:** Keep the product dark-only with deep black and charcoal surfaces, electric purple and teal accents, restrained neon signal details, balanced information density, high-contrast typography, rounded geometry, subtle abstract African-inspired angular language, and Lwazi as a minimal digital knowledge spirit. Prioritize responsive dashboard, onboarding, settings, empty/loading/error, and public landing-page surfaces.
